The public component registry provides this vanilla engine for copying into applications.
Local adaptations: low-power WebGL preference , 1.5x raster limit, bounded gaze and stationary reduced-motion behavior, application conversation-state gaze, and mouse-only pointer steering (touch keeps autonomous idle gaze). No React wrapper or external runtime assets.
Native Windows rendering is a separate WinUI adaptation of the sphere and capsule-eye visual, not the WebGL engine.
